# Luxury Exterior Visualization

## Purpose

Create a photorealistic architectural visualization of an approved luxury property exterior without changing the building design.

## Prompt template

Create a high-end photorealistic architectural visualization of this luxury property exterior.

Preserve the approved building design, massing, façade, number of floors, windows, doors, balconies, roofline, materials, landscape layout, surrounding context, and camera position.

Improve environmental realism, vegetation, sky, reflections, shadows, depth, material detail, and atmospheric perspective.

**Property type:** [residence / condominium / hotel / commercial]  
**Lighting:** [daylight / sunset / blue hour / evening]  
**Weather:** [clear / soft clouds / tropical atmosphere]  
**Camera:** [lens, height, angle, and composition]  
**Output:** [aspect ratio and resolution]

Use straight architectural lines, physically believable lighting, realistic vegetation, controlled highlights, and natural material response.

Do not invent floors, windows, balconies, entrances, amenities, views, neighboring buildings, or structural features.

Avoid distorted geometry, unrealistic vegetation, dramatic artificial skies, excessive glow, oversaturation, fake HDR, repeated objects, and visible AI artifacts.

## Quality gate

- Building geometry matches the approved source.
- Floor count, façade, windows, balconies, and roofline remain unchanged.
- Perspective and vertical lines are accurate.
- Landscaping, reflections, shadows, and materials are believable.
- No unapproved structures, amenities, views, or surrounding elements are present.
