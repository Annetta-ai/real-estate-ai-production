# Image → Cinematic Video

## Purpose

Transform an approved real-estate image into a short cinematic video while preserving architectural stability.

## Prompt template

Create a cinematic real-estate video from the provided image.

Preserve the architecture, geometry, composition, materials, furniture, skyline, landscaping, lighting direction, and object placement exactly as shown.

Use a slow, controlled [dolly-in / dolly-out / lateral slide / subtle orbit] with realistic depth, gentle parallax, natural motion blur, and stable professional camera behavior.

**Duration:** [number] seconds  
**Aspect ratio:** [16:9 / 9:16 / 4:5]  
**Camera movement:** [movement type and direction]  
**Camera speed:** very slow and stable  
**Focus:** [fixed / gentle focus transition]  
**Output:** [resolution and frame rate]

Do not redesign the property. No structural morphing, flicker, texture drift, sudden zoom, camera shake, bending lines, moving walls, disappearing objects, newly generated objects, or excessive environmental movement.

Fixed architectural elements must remain stable from the first frame to the last.

## Quality gate

- Architecture remains consistent throughout the clip.
- Camera movement is smooth and physically believable.
- Straight lines and fixed objects remain stable.
- No flicker, morphing, texture drift, or unintended movement is visible.
- The first and final frames match the approved source.
