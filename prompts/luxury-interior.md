# Luxury Interior Visualization

## Purpose

Create a photorealistic editorial visualization of an approved luxury interior without changing its architecture or design.

## Prompt template

Create a high-end photorealistic editorial visualization of this luxury interior.

Preserve the approved architecture, room dimensions, layout, ceiling height, windows, doors, built-in elements, furniture placement, materials, finishes, and camera perspective.

Improve natural lighting, texture detail, reflections, depth, material realism, and atmosphere. Use soft directional light, controlled highlights, realistic shadows, accurate material response, and restrained luxury styling.

**Space:** [living room / bedroom / kitchen / lobby / other]  
**Lighting:** [daylight / sunset / evening]  
**Camera:** [lens, height, angle, and composition]  
**Style:** [quiet luxury / contemporary / minimalist / editorial]  
**Output:** [aspect ratio and resolution]

The result must feel sophisticated, believable, and professionally photographed.

Avoid generic luxury clichés, excessive decoration, distorted furniture, incorrect scale, plastic materials, oversaturated colors, artificial HDR, repeated objects, books used as generic decoration, clay vases, and unnecessary styling elements.

## Quality gate

- Architecture, layout, and furniture placement match the approved source.
- Furniture and decorative objects have believable scale.
- Materials, lighting, shadows, and reflections behave naturally.
- Vertical and horizontal lines remain accurate.
- No unapproved design elements or visible AI artifacts are present.
