# CGI → Photorealistic Image

## Purpose

Transform approved architectural CGI into a high-end photorealistic real-estate image without redesigning the property.

## Prompt template

Transform this architectural CGI into a high-end photorealistic real-estate photograph.

Preserve the original architecture, geometry, proportions, materials, camera position, perspective, composition, openings, structural details, and surrounding context. Do not redesign, crop, extend, remove, or move architectural elements.

Improve natural lighting, material texture, reflections, shadows, landscaping, depth, and atmospheric realism. Maintain realistic scale and physically believable light behavior.

**Property type:** [interior / exterior / residential / commercial]  
**Lighting:** [daylight / sunset / blue hour / evening]  
**Camera:** [lens, camera height, and composition]  
**Atmosphere:** [clean / warm / cinematic / editorial]  
**Output:** [aspect ratio and resolution]

Avoid warped lines, altered geometry, plastic materials, repeated objects, floating details, artificial HDR, excessive sharpness, oversaturation, and visible AI artifacts.

## Quality gate

- Architecture matches the approved source.
- Perspective and vertical lines remain accurate.
- Materials, reflections, shadows, landscaping, and scale are believable.
- No elements have been added, removed, duplicated, or distorted.
- The image reads as professional real-estate photography at 100% zoom.
