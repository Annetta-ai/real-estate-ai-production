# CGI → Photorealistic Image → Cinematic Video

## 1. Source intake

Collect:

- Approved architectural CGI
- Plans and architectural references
- Material and landscaping references
- Client requirements
- Required aspect ratios, resolutions, and deadlines

Confirm which elements must remain unchanged.

## 2. Image preparation

- Select the approved CGI view.
- Check perspective, geometry, materials, and image resolution.
- Remove labels or overlays only when approved.
- Save an untouched copy of the original source.

## 3. Prompt development

- Select the CGI-to-photorealistic prompt template.
- Define lighting, atmosphere, camera characteristics, and output format.
- Add explicit preservation constraints.
- Record the model, tool, settings, and prompt version.

## 4. Image generation

- Generate several controlled variations.
- Compare every variation with the approved CGI.
- Select the result with the strongest realism and architectural accuracy.
- Refine only the areas that fail review.

## 5. Image approval

Apply the image quality checklist.

Do not continue to video until the image passes the quality gate and receives human approval.

## 6. Video planning

Define:

- Shot duration
- Aspect ratio
- Camera movement
- Movement direction
- Camera speed
- Focus behavior
- Delivery resolution and frame rate

Use one simple camera movement per shot.

## 7. Video generation

- Generate a short test clip.
- Preserve all fixed architectural elements.
- Keep environmental movement subtle and believable.
- Avoid unnecessary motion that may destabilize the scene.

## 8. Video approval

Review the complete clip and inspect critical moments frame by frame.

Reject clips with:

- Flicker
- Structural morphing
- Texture drift
- Unstable lines
- Camera shake
- Sudden zoom
- New or disappearing objects

## 9. Export and archive

- Export the approved master file.
- Create required web, social, and presentation versions.
- Use clear filenames with project, scene, format, and version.
- Archive sources, references, prompts, settings, working versions, and final assets.
