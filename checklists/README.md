# Quality Checklists

## Available checklists

- [Image Quality Checklist](image-quality.md)
- [Video Quality Checklist](video-quality.md)

## Image quality gate

- Architecture and geometry match the approved source.
- Perspective and vertical lines remain accurate.
- Materials, shadows, reflections, and scale are believable.
- No warped lines, repeated objects, floating details, or visible AI artifacts.
- Output meets the required aspect ratio and resolution.

## Video quality gate

- Fixed architectural elements remain stable.
- Camera movement is slow, controlled, and physically believable.
- No flicker, morphing, texture drift, camera shake, or sudden zoom.
- No new, disappearing, or unintentionally moving objects.
- First and final frames remain consistent with the approved image.

## Final approval

- Prompts, models, tools, settings, and versions are documented.
- Files follow the approved naming and delivery structure.
- Client-facing assets receive human approval before delivery or publication.
