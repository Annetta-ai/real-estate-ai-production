# Video Quality Checklist

Use this checklist before approving any image-to-video real-estate sequence.

## Architecture

- [ ] Building geometry remains unchanged throughout the clip.
- [ ] Walls, windows, doors, ceilings, furniture, and fixed objects remain stable.
- [ ] Straight architectural lines do not bend or move.
- [ ] No objects appear, disappear, duplicate, or change shape.
- [ ] The first and final frames remain consistent with the approved source.

## Camera movement

- [ ] Camera movement is slow, smooth, and controlled.
- [ ] Movement direction matches the approved shot plan.
- [ ] Depth and parallax are physically believable.
- [ ] No sudden zoom, camera shake, acceleration, or unintended rotation.
- [ ] Motion blur is natural and restrained.

## Visual stability

- [ ] No flicker, morphing, texture drift, or pulsing light.
- [ ] Materials and reflections remain consistent.
- [ ] Landscaping, water, clouds, and people move naturally.
- [ ] Environmental motion does not destabilize the architecture.
- [ ] No visible AI artifacts appear in individual frames.

## Technical quality

- [ ] Duration, aspect ratio, resolution, and frame rate are correct.
- [ ] The full clip was reviewed from beginning to end.
- [ ] Critical moments were inspected frame by frame.
- [ ] Prompt, model, tool, settings, source image, and version are documented.
- [ ] Exported file plays correctly without compression artifacts.

## Approval

- [ ] Video passed human review.
- [ ] Architecture matches the approved image or CGI.
- [ ] Asset is cleared for client delivery or publication.
