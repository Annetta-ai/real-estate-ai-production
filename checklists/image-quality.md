# Image Quality Checklist

Use this checklist before approving any CGI-to-photorealistic real-estate image.

## Architecture

- [ ] Geometry matches the approved source.
- [ ] Walls, floors, ceilings, windows, doors, balconies, and rooflines remain unchanged.
- [ ] No architectural elements were added, removed, duplicated, or moved.
- [ ] Perspective and vertical lines are accurate.
- [ ] Room, furniture, and landscape scale is believable.

## Materials and lighting

- [ ] Materials match the approved design.
- [ ] Textures have realistic scale and detail.
- [ ] Reflections correspond with the surrounding scene.
- [ ] Shadows follow one believable lighting direction.
- [ ] Glass, metal, stone, wood, and fabric behave naturally.
- [ ] Highlights are controlled without artificial HDR.

## Environment and styling

- [ ] Landscaping and vegetation look natural.
- [ ] Skyline, water, neighboring buildings, and views remain accurate.
- [ ] Furniture and decorative objects are not distorted or duplicated.
- [ ] No unapproved property features or generic decorative clutter were added.

## Technical quality

- [ ] No warped lines, floating objects, repeated patterns, or visible AI artifacts.
- [ ] No excessive sharpening, smoothing, glow, or oversaturation.
- [ ] Required aspect ratio and resolution are correct.
- [ ] Image remains convincing at 100% zoom.
- [ ] Prompt, model, tool, settings, and version are documented.

## Approval

- [ ] Final image was compared with the original CGI and references.
- [ ] Architectural accuracy was approved by a human reviewer.
- [ ] Asset is cleared for client delivery or publication.
